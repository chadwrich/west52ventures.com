About

West 52 Ventures is a software research and development company focused on building practical, well-crafted products for real-world challenges.

We explore ideas at the intersection of technology, usability, and human needs—rapidly prototyping, testing, and refining products that are designed to be understandable, practical, and trustworthy.

Our work emphasizes clarity over complexity, privacy-conscious design, and building tools that people can actually use. We take ideas from early research through to working software, validating them with real users before moving to production.

Work

BumperBingo

BumperBingo is a premium family road trip game that transforms license plate spotting into a collectible educational experience. Spot plates from all 50 states, collect beautifully illustrated plate cards, answer trivia, and compete with everyone in the car.

Designed for families with players ages 6 and up. Fully offline, zero data collection, and built with original artwork in a vintage road-trip poster style.
