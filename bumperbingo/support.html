Getting Started

BumperBingo is a family road trip game where you collect license plates from all 50 states. Spot a plate, tap the state, collect a beautiful plate card, answer trivia, and earn badges.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a trip?

Tap "Create Trip" on the home screen, name your trip, select players, and you're on the road. Everyone starts at zero points.

Can multiple people play on one device?

Yes! Add up to 10 players to a trip. Each player tracks their own finds and competes on the leaderboard.

Does BumperBingo need an internet connection?

No. BumperBingo works completely offline. All content is stored on your device. The only network activity is for optional in-app purchases through Apple.

How is my data stored?

All game data stays on your device. We don't collect any personal information, and there are no accounts or logins. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What ages is BumperBingo for?

BumperBingo is rated 4+ and designed for families. Players ages 6 and up can enjoy the game independently, while younger children can play with help from an adult.
