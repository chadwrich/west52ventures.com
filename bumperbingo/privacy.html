BumperBingo Privacy Policy

West 52 Ventures LLC

Effective Date: July 1, 2025

Summary

BumperBingo does not collect any personal data. Your privacy is important to us, and we have designed our app to work entirely on your device without gathering or transmitting personal information.

Data Collection

  • No personal information is collected
  • No user accounts or login required
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No advertising or ad-related data collection

Data Storage

All game data — including your plate collection, trip history, scores, and badges — is stored exclusively on your device. We do not have access to your data, and it is never uploaded to any server.

Network Communication

The only network communication BumperBingo performs is through Apple's StoreKit 2 framework for optional in-app purchases. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple and governed by Apple's own privacy policy. BumperBingo does not operate any servers or make any other network requests.

Children's Privacy

BumperBingo is designed for families and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else. Because the app collects no data at all, there is no risk of inadvertent collection of children's information.

Third-Party Services

BumperBingo does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only third-party integration is Apple's StoreKit 2 for in-app purchases, which is managed by Apple.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal information to access, correct, or delete. Your game data remains on your device and under your control at all times.

Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please contact us at: privacy@west52ventures.com

West 52 Ventures LLC

Changes to This Policy

We will update this page if our privacy practices change. Any changes will be reflected by an updated effective date at the top of this policy.
